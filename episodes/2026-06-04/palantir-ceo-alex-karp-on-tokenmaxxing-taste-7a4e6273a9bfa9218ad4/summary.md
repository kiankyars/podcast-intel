# Palantir CEO Alex Karp on Tokenmaxxing & Taste

- Podcast: TBPN
- Published: 2026-06-04
- Source: https://share.transistor.fm/s/14a1df49
- Relevance: 4/5

Alex Karp gives a characteristically blunt Palantir thesis: enterprises are tired of tokenmaxxing, model intelligence is becoming more commoditized, and the scarce layer is taste plus ontology embedded in real workflows. The transcript is messy and lacks usable timestamps, but the core first-party signal is clear: Palantir wants to be judged by deployed enterprise outcomes rather than raw AI consumption.

**Why it matters:** The episode captures how Palantir is positioning itself against both frontier labs and generic enterprise AI vendors. Karp's framing is that durable value comes from problem selection, workflow ontology, deployment credibility, and political navigation, not just better LLMs.
