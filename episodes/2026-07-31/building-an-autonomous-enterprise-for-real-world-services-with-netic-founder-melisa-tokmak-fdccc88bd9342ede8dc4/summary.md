# Building an Autonomous Enterprise for Real-World Services with Netic Founder Melisa Tokmak

- Podcast: No Priors
- Published: 2026-07-31
- Source: 
- Relevance: 4/5

Netic founder and CEO Melisa Tokmak describes an AI operations layer for essential-services enterprises that handles customer intake, operational reasoning, and labor dispatch. She reports that more than 70% of Netic customers now put the agents first in their customer journey, cites a roughly $500,000 contract closed in 14 days, and says AI-handled interactions have generated more than $600 million for customers. Those commercial figures are company-reported and the episode does not provide denominators, time periods, or an incrementality methodology.

**Why it matters:** The episode offers concrete evidence that vertical agents are moving beyond overflow support into the primary interface for complex, revenue-linked physical-service workflows. It also frames the application company's moat as domain data, harnesses, orchestration, integrations, and enterprise reliability rather than proprietary access to a frontier model.

- **Tokmak chose a scalable product company over an AI roll-up because she believes software built inside an acquired operator remains captive to those assets, while a shared intelligence layer can compound across many independent service businesses.** [09:21] _companies_capital_allocation; opinion; medium confidence._ She says roll-up economics depend heavily on M&A and constrain product deployment to purchased companies, whereas Netic is intended to run the non-labor operations of many businesses across industries.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- Vertical voice agents can become the default customer interface for complex physical-service enterprises, not just an overflow channel.
- The defensible unit in vertical AI may be the operational harness and integrated workflow layer rather than a proprietary model.
- Essential-services enterprises may adopt revenue-linked AI faster than their reputation for slow technology procurement implies.
