# 90 minutes of unfiltered product advice from Snap and Discord’s product chief | Peter Sellis

- Podcast: Lenny's Podcast
- Published: 2026-09-20
- Source: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/90-minutes-of-unfiltered-product
- Relevance: 4/5

Former Snap and Discord product leader Peter Sellis offers useful first-party evidence on consumer monetization and AI adoption. He says Midjourney's prominence did not produce a discernible change in Discord's overall usage trajectory, while a 2024 refocus on multiplayer gaming and performance produced unusually strong post-pandemic growth. He attributes Snap's advertising constraints to its young audience, camera-first interaction and messaging utility, and describes slow subscription conversion as another structural consideration. His most concrete AI adoption example is jointly querying Hex with Discord's cofounder during meetings. He also proposes pricing the cost to user trust into conversational advertising; this is an outside operator's recommendation, not an OpenAI implementation disclosure.

**Why it matters:** Sellis separates visible AI activity from material platform growth and explains how product interaction patterns constrain monetization even at large scale. His Hex example identifies a specific collaborative workflow where AI changed executive behavior, while his advertising analysis suggests that chat monetization depends on retention and trust as well as auction infrastructure. The evidence is qualitative and retrospective: he provides no quantified growth attribution, revenue lift, subscription conversion curves or measured AI productivity gains.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- Sellis's account weakens the assumption that hosting a highly visible AI community necessarily moves a large platform's overall growth; frequency gains in the existing core may matter more.
- Snap's monetization limitations deserve to be analyzed as interaction-design and inventory constraints as well as demographic or execution problems.
- The Hex example supports collaborative, in-meeting data exploration as a concrete AI adoption pattern, while leaving its accuracy, scale and economic return unmeasured.

## Follow-Ups

- Look for Discord usage cohorts and feature-level evidence that quantify the contribution of its 2024 gaming and performance refocus separately from market growth and AI communities.
- For collaborative analytics products such as Hex, track query correctness, response latency, repeat team usage and whether live answers shorten consequential decisions.
- Compare paid conversion by user tenure for Snapchat Plus and Discord Nitro to test the proposed delay between establishing a messaging habit and valuing a subscription.
- Watch for chat-ad experiments reporting both incremental advertiser value and retention or trust effects; distinguish observed results from Sellis's proposed auction mechanism.
