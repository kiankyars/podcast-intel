# 🔬 The Self-Driving Lab — Joseph Krause, Radical AI

- Podcast: Latent Space
- Published: 2026-06-17
- Source: https://www.latent.space/p/radical-ai
- Relevance: 4/5

Joseph Krause and Radical AI describe a self-driving lab approach to materials discovery that relies on closed-loop experimental data rather than one-shot model prediction. The concrete claims are that Radical produced and characterized 1,200 alloys in six months, sees a path to 100 tested alloys per day, and found 10 novel state-of-the-art materials from 300 AI-proposed candidates.

**Why it matters:** The episode is useful because it gives operational numbers for AI-for-science throughput, not just abstract claims. It also frames experimental data and automated lab infrastructure as the moat, which is directly relevant to materials for aerospace, defense, computing, and supply-chain resilience.
