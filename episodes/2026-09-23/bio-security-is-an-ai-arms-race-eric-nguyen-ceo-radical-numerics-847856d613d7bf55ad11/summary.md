# 🔬Bio-security is an AI Arms Race - Eric Nguyen (CEO, Radical Numerics)

- Podcast: Latent Space
- Published: 2026-09-23
- Source: https://www.latent.space/p/bio-security-is-an-ai-arms-race-eric
- Relevance: 4/5

Radical Numerics CEO Eric Nguyen describes Omni as a move from genomic base models to tools scientists can use, emphasizing task-specific mid-training and post-training. He reports stronger human-genomics benchmarks but distinguishes unsupervised pretraining from supervised task adaptation. The interview also exposes the limits of the current preview: biological reasoning results still await wet-lab validation, broad multimodal integration is a roadmap, and biosecurity tools are intended to improve layered defense rather than guarantee prevention. A reported national-lab collaboration on rare-earth extraction broadens the potential application set beyond health.

**Why it matters:** The consequential shift is from demonstrating genomic generation to delivering usable scientific workflows and measuring their real-world value. Nguyen's account puts task adaptation, experimental validation and biological data integration at the center of that transition. His defense strategy also creates a separate potential user base among surveillance and response organizations. The interview establishes company research priorities and reported results, not clinical efficacy, industrial extraction economics or demonstrated protection against all biological threats.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- The Omni account strengthens the case that task adaptation and usable interfaces, rather than raw genomic pretraining alone, will determine near-term scientific adoption. Reported benchmark gains still need comparable training regimes and independent validation.
- The rare-earth research engagement expands the potential market beyond medical applications, but it is too early to infer a materials-production business or strategic supply contribution.
- Radical Numerics describes biosecurity as a continuing service to a wider defense ecosystem; model-level safeguards alone are not the full intended product boundary.
