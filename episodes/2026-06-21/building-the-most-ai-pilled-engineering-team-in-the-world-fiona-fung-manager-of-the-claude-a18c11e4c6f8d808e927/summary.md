# Building the most AI-pilled engineering team in the world | Fiona Fung (Manager of the Claude Code and Cowork Teams)

- Podcast: Lenny's Podcast
- Published: 2026-06-21
- Source: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/building-the-most-ai-pilled-engineering
- Relevance: 4/5

Fiona Fung gives a first-party operating view from Anthropic's Claude Code and Cowork teams. The strongest signal is not generic AI enthusiasm but concrete workflow change: Anthropic engineers are shipping about 8x more code, non-engineering roles are checking in code, management review is being rebuilt around Claude-accessible repos, Slack, metrics, and feedback, and planning cycles are shortening because six-month roadmaps decay too quickly.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct look at how one frontier lab is reorganizing software work around coding agents. It suggests the next bottlenecks are verification, management visibility, async agent orchestration, role boundaries, and process design rather than raw code generation alone.

## Signals

- **Fung and Rachitsky say Anthropic engineers now ship roughly 8x as much code per quarter as they did from 2021 to 2025.** [03:33] _agents_developer_tools; observation; high confidence._ The episode opens and the show notes cite the 8x figure, then tie it to a shift where designers, PMs, and other disciplines are also checking in code.
- **Fung says an AI-pilled software team is becoming a builder organization where roles blur and verification becomes central.** [09:20] _agents_developer_tools; observation; high confidence._ She says everybody starts being a builder, while the host frames the problem as high throughput from multiple disciplines requiring confidence that the code works.
- **Fung uses a Claude Code remote session across repos, Slack, metrics, and feedback channels as a management visibility layer.** [10:13] _agents_developer_tools; observation; high confidence._ She describes a session enlisted in all repos with access to Slack and tracked metrics, used to review focus areas, shipped products, market response, feedback, bugs, and quality hotspots.
- **Fung says Claude-supported management review changes evaluation from output volume to impact, quality, and new mistake patterns.** [11:20] _companies_capital_allocation; observation; high confidence._ She says the monthly review asks how shipped work performed in market, whether it caused bugs, where incident themes suggest investment, and encourages making new mistakes rather than aiming for zero mistakes.
- **Fung says the next engineering shift is asynchronous agent work through routines that can spawn other agents and produce PRs for review.** [35:36] _agents_developer_tools; forecast; high confidence._ She describes scheduled routines that inspect feedback, identify polish fixes, kick off agents, and leave PRs waiting when she wakes up, with more autonomy possible where verification is strong.
- **Fung says PMs and other coding-adjacent roles are being transformed because they are no longer bottlenecked on engineering bandwidth.** [1:00:23] _applications_business_models; observation; high confidence._ She says PMs have rolled up their sleeves to ship features when an engineer was unavailable, while design and data science are becoming the next workflow-automation opportunities.
- **Fung says Claude Code/Cowork team culture gives explicit permission to kill obsolete processes and has moved from six-month roadmaps to just-in-time planning.** [1:23:03] _companies_capital_allocation; observation; high confidence._ She says a lightweight six-month roadmap stopped being useful after about three months because the field changed so quickly, so she now uses JIT planning.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- At Anthropic, coding agents are already changing management practice and product review, not just individual engineering throughput.
- Verification and quality processes are becoming the limiting surface once designers, PMs, and other non-engineers can submit code.
- Scheduled agent routines may turn management and product operations into asynchronous agent orchestration workflows.
- Longer product planning cycles may be too stale for fast-moving AI-native product teams.
