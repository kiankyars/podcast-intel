# NVIDIA Won’t Be the Same Without Jensen Huang — Tae Kim

- Podcast: TBPN
- Published: 2026-11-13
- Source: https://share.transistor.fm/s/6f9b7193
- Relevance: 3/5

Tae Kim offers a conditional path for Nvidia after Jensen Huang's departure: retain its senior technical and financial leaders, give them clear authority and choose a CEO who complements their capabilities. He separates the next two to three years of products from strategic decisions that could matter three to five years out. These are succession judgments, not evidence of executive departures, completed retention packages or a changed roadmap. This clip duplicates the Tae Kim segment in the November 13 full TBPN show; findings appear only here, with clip timestamps.

**Why it matters:** Kim adds a specific counterpoint to yesterday's concern that Nvidia could lose Huang's ability to coordinate decisions: distributed technical leadership might preserve execution if key people stay and receive real authority. That makes retention and decision rights observable tests of the transition. Near-term product delivery alone would not resolve the longer-term strategic question he raises.
