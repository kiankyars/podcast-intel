# OpenAI’s Head of ChatGPT: We’re entering a new era of AI (again) | Tibo Sottiaux

- Podcast: Lenny's Podcast
- Published: 2026-10-04
- Source: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/openais-head-of-chatgpt-were-entering
- Relevance: 4/5

In a DevDay interview published October 4, OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux adds commercial detail beyond the earlier product announcements: he describes shared economics for popular plugins and external applications using Sign in with ChatGPT, and says retention and utility affect in-conversation recommendations. He also reports that Notion's MCP integration brought enough agent traffic to create infrastructure and economic pressure. His own agent workflow changes as latency and model capability improve, complicating any assumption that larger agent teams are always better. The broader vision of permanent assistants, product convergence and simpler interfaces repeats earlier coverage; multi-Dot teams remain a future release in his account, and his production-outage anecdote establishes an alert followed by human intervention, not autonomous repair.

**Why it matters:** The most consequential addition is the connection between distribution, compensation and the cost of serving agent-driven demand. A developer could gain both recommended placement and shared revenue, but its economics still depend on undisclosed payout rules, retained usage and the workload imposed on its own systems. Separately, faster or stronger models can change the useful division of work between agents, so orchestration overhead and task outcomes need to be measured at each capability level.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- The earlier portable-subscription announcement mainly changed who funds inference. This interview adds an explicit plan to compensate participating plugins and external products, while leaving payout mechanics unresolved.
- Agent access can increase application distribution and backend workload together. Retention and revenue per completed workflow matter more than raw agent traffic.
- Agent-team size is not a stable proxy for capability: latency and context improvements can make previously useful parallel decomposition unnecessary for a particular user and task.

## Follow-Ups

- Look for published partner compensation terms: eligible integrations, revenue basis, usage attribution, payout schedule and whether external Sign in with ChatGPT products use the same rules as plugins.
- Track documented recommendation metrics and controlled evidence connecting retention or successful task completion to plugin exposure.
- Seek a first-party Notion account quantifying MCP traffic, infrastructure cost and revenue before inferring a positive or negative margin effect.
- Compare a single fast agent with coordinated agents on the same workloads, measuring completed-task quality, elapsed time, total tokens and human intervention.
