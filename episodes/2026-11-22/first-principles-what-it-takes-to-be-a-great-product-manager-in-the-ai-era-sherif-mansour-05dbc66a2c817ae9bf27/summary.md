# First principles: What it takes to be a great product manager in the AI era | Sherif Mansour

- Podcast: Lenny's Podcast
- Published: 2026-11-22
- Source: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/first-principles-what-it-takes-to
- Relevance: 4/5

Atlassian’s Sherif Mansour describes what happened when a few squads put product managers and designers into a common AI builder role. One participant reported faster early output, then a return to specialist work as the team struggled to choose what to build. Mansour also explains how customers’ experiments with Rovo have shaped dedicated features in Jira and Confluence.

**Why it matters:** Atlassian provides examples from both sides of enterprise AI adoption. Inside the company, faster implementation exposed the cost of agreeing on a direction. In the product, existing customer workflows gave the team evidence about which agent tasks merited a dedicated interface.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- A general chat interface can serve as a discovery tool even when the final interaction is a visual selection or an automated step inside a workflow.
