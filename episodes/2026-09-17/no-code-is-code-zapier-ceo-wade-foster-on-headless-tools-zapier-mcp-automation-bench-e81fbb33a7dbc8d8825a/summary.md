# No Code Is Code: Zapier CEO Wade Foster on Headless Tools, Zapier MCP & Automation Bench

- Podcast: The Cognitive Revolution
- Published: 2026-09-17
- Source: https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/no-code-is-code-zapier-ceo-wade-foster-on-headless-tools-zapier-mcp-automation-bench/
- Relevance: 4/5

Wade Foster describes Zapier moving into users’ preferred AI tools through MCP. His proposed architecture uses agents to build and repair workflows while deterministic code handles repeatable execution. He reports weak end-to-end enterprise-task performance even from frontier models and describes concrete support-evaluation, workflow-discovery and token-cost practices inside Zapier.

**Why it matters:** The useful application-layer opportunity is turning model reasoning into reliable business processes with context, integration and feedback. Foster provides operational examples, but does not yet quantify the benchmark lift or savings delivered by Zapier’s tools.

## Signals

- **Foster estimates that about 80% of customers’ agent usage would be better served by deterministic code.** [13:46; 18:02] _agents_developer_tools; opinion; medium confidence._ He advocates agents building and repairing workflows, with AI reserved for steps requiring reasoning. Visual workflows remain useful for human verification. This is his estimate, not a published task-by-task audit.
- **Foster reports roughly 40% success for Astra on about 600 business tasks; Zapier’s incremental lift remains unreported here.** [10:21; 13:46; 23:29] _agents_developer_tools; observation; medium confidence._ Automation Bench spans sales, marketing, HR and operations. Asked for measured improvements with Zapier tools, he defers to a future V2 release. The result is benchmark-specific, not a general workplace success rate.
- **Zapier’s support troubleshooting program runs five independent agents and uses human rejection reasons to improve the system.** [24:30; 29:05] _agents_developer_tools; observation; high confidence._ Foster says agreement by four agents is a useful diagnostic signal. Human auditors approve or reject outputs with explanations; he gives no accuracy or cost comparison against a single agent.
- **Foster describes weekly activity-based automation recommendations as an internal adoption mechanism.** [37:07; 39:41; 40:58] _applications_business_models; observation; high confidence._ Connected-tool event histories prompt suggested workflows that employees accept or refine. He expects a customer product, but does not announce its availability or conversion results.
- **Zapier is using spend visibility and coaching before imposing individual AI-token budgets.** [54:53; 55:46] _companies_capital_allocation; observation; high confidence._ Foster acknowledges very high spenders as outliers. Dashboards and conversations distinguish productive usage from unnecessarily expensive model choices; differentiated budgets remain a future expectation.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- Agent adoption does not imply that every workflow step should execute through a model; deterministic execution can remain the efficient endpoint of agent-authored automation.
- A vendor’s benchmark and its product’s incremental improvement are separate claims: this interview supplies the former but postpones the latter.
