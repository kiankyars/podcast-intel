# OpenAI DevDay | Sam Altman, Alexander Embiricos, Andrew Ambrosino, Peter Steinberger, Tibo Sottiaux, Kath Korevec, Casey Neistat

- Published: 2026-09-29

- Relevance: 4/5

OpenAI's DevDay interviews provide first-party detail on the economics and architecture of persistent agents. Sam Altman describes taking a ChatGPT subscription into outside applications and targets the first half of 2027 for OpenAI's chip program to start coming online. Tibo Sottiaux attributes a reported tenfold computer-use speedup to model, reviewer and harness changes, identifies the shared Codex harness behind Dots and managed agents, and explains employee-like identity provisioning for specialist agents. Alexander Embiricos and Andrew Ambrosino distinguish personal delegates, organizational identities and cloud execution with local access. Kath Korevec reports 10 million Sites in roughly three months; creation volume does not establish retention or revenue. Broad capability superlatives, promotional demos and host speculation are not retained as verified outcomes.

**Why it matters:** The consequential changes are who pays for inference, which identity authorizes an agent, and how execution and safety checks fit into the latency budget. Portable subscription access could reduce application developers' direct inference bills, while agent identities and shared execution infrastructure make persistent workers more deployable inside organizations. These interviews add implementation detail to the previously stated product-convergence strategy, but do not establish unlimited compute, reliable autonomous performance, measured customer savings or the promised chip efficiency advantage.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- The August 30 Tara Seshan interview established product convergence and hosted personal software as directions. This episode adds a named shared harness, a described cloud/local execution arrangement and a Sites creation count; it does not demonstrate that the full convergence roadmap is finished.
- Following the September 26 discussion of cheap heartbeat routing and September 29 estimate of expensive personal-agent serving, subscription portability changes which party can fund inference. It does not by itself reduce the underlying compute cost or establish profitable unit economics.
- Computer-use speed should be assessed across primary-model reasoning, action review and orchestration. Sottiaux's account supplies a mechanism for progress beyond faster sampling, while leaving both benchmark comparability and safety tradeoffs unquantified.

## Follow-Ups

- Verify Sign in with ChatGPT eligibility, supported applications and models, allowance sharing, rate limits and overage handling before modeling an application developer's economics.
- Compare reproducible computer-use latency and task-success measurements with and without Guardian and each harness optimization; do not trade away authorization checks to reproduce a headline speed claim.
- Inspect specialist-agent identity lifecycle, connector scopes, audit logs and revocation, including the boundary between cloud execution and authorized local access.
- Track the chip program's first operational capacity and measured workload-specific performance per watt against the first-half-2027 forecast.
- Separate Sites creation from active usage, retention, hosting costs and revenue; watch for documented plugin permissions and any confirmed free-tier availability.
