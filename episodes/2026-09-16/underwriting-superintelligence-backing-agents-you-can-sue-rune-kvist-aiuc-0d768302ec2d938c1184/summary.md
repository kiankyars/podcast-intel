# Underwriting Superintelligence: Backing Agents you can Sue — Rune Kvist, AIUC

- Podcast: Latent Space
- Published: 2026-09-16
- Source: https://www.latent.space/p/aiuc
- Relevance: 4/5

AIUC cofounder Rune Kvist reports a $40 million round and work with Cursor, Harvey, Lovable and ElevenLabs. He explains how recurring agent tests, external control audits and insurer-backed policies fit together, while acknowledging that claims experience is absent and some risks remain difficult to insure.

**Why it matters:** Enterprise agent deployment increasingly requires evidence buyers can use in their own risk processes. AIUC offers a concrete commercial model for supplying that evidence, but certification is neither continuous verification nor proof that losses will be covered. Its underwriting economics and testing limits matter more than the broad trust narrative.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- The interview makes AI assurance more concrete: a recurring technical-testing business can support enterprise procurement and premium sharing without a startup supplying most of the insurance capital. The lack of claims experience still limits evidence that this model will work through losses.
