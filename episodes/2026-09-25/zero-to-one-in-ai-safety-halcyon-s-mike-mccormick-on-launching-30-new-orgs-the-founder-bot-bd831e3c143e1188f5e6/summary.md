# Zero to One in AI Safety: Halcyon's Mike McCormick on Launching 30 New Orgs & the Founder Bottleneck

- Podcast: The Cognitive Revolution
- Published: 2026-09-25
- Source: https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/zero-to-one-in-ai-safety-halcyon-s-mike-mccormick-on-launching-30-new-orgs-the-founder-bottleneck/
- Relevance: 3/5

Halcyon founder Mike McCormick describes an organization-building model that combines career-transition grants with subsequent nonprofit funding or venture investment. He reports roughly 30 organizations launched over three years and about $500 million raised collectively, and explains how grants can support founders before they select a project. The most useful additions are early investments in verifiable inference and compute infrastructure, a focus on projects that could matter within one to four years, and demand for experienced operating leaders. The AIUC product overview repeats September 17 coverage; broad catastrophe timelines, consciousness speculation and prospective safety-market valuations are not retained as new evidence.

**Why it matters:** The interview identifies a concrete allocation mechanism for expanding the independent AI safety ecosystem: finance experienced people through project selection, then help them recruit and launch. It also connects verification research to a potential infrastructure requirement for enforcing model and compute commitments. Halcyon's reported organization count and aggregate fundraising describe ecosystem formation, not its own assets under management, investment returns or demonstrated reductions in risk. McCormick is an interested funder; product maturity, grant conversion rates and the relative severity of capital versus talent constraints remain unmeasured here.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- The account provides a specific mechanism for creating new safety organizations before conventional seed funding: support an experienced person's transition and project search, then supply organizational help and subsequent capital. It does not establish that talent is the binding constraint for every part of AI safety.
- Verification is presented as an emerging compute-infrastructure investment category as well as a research and policy problem. Funded attempts strengthen evidence of formation, while implementation cost, adoption and enforceability remain open.
