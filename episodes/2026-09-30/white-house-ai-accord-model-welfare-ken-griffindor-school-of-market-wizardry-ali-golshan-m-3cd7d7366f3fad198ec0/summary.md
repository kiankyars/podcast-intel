Founder and executive interviews add detail on NVIDIA’s agent containment architecture, CoreWeave’s capacity-pricing thesis, Robinhood’s agentic trading adoption, Flow’s hardware-verification workflow and EliseAI’s commercial scale. External runtime policy enforcement offers a concrete containment mechanism but does not establish universal security. The commercial metrics require care: agent account openings are not active usage, hardware workflow adoption is not proof of automated verification, and EliseAI’s apartment-coverage claims use unresolved competing figures.

**Why it matters:** The useful evidence concerns enforcement outside the model, infrastructure procurement horizons and adoption within existing workflows. September coverage already addressed older-GPU demand, ClusterMAX rankings and agent failures; this episode adds implementation details and first-party operating claims. Revenue, account creation and product penetration remain distinct from profitability, retention and reliable autonomous performance.
