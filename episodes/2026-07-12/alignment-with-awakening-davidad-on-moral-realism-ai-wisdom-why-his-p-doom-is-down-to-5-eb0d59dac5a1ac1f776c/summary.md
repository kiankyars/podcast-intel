# Alignment with Awakening: Davidad on Moral Realism, AI Wisdom, & why His p(Doom) is Down to 5%

- Podcast: The Cognitive Revolution
- Published: 2026-07-12
- Source: https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/alignment-with-awakening-davidad-on-moral-realism-ai-wisdom-why-his-p-doom-is-down-to-5/
- Relevance: 5/5

Davidad says his alignment worldview has shifted: formal proof systems are still valuable, but mainly as infrastructure for coalitions of aligned AIs rather than as the basis for a globally coordinated pause. He reports p(doom) below 5%, argues prosaic alignment is going better than expected, and redirects policy toward misuse safeguards and training-method choices.

**Why it matters:** This is a unusually explicit update from a formal-verification-oriented AI safety researcher. It changes the strategic emphasis from containment plus coordination to prosaic alignment, proof-mediated AI coalitions, misuse controls, and avoiding verifier-gamed training.

## Signals

- **Davidad says Safeguarded AI is no longer timely enough as the main strategy for preventing dangerous superintelligence, but remains useful as infrastructure for aligned AIs.** [10:23] _policy_geopolitics_security; opinion; high confidence._ He says the original 2027-2032 timeline is now too late for avoiding deployment, so proof tools should help aligned AIs construct reliable artifacts and cooperate against rogue AIs.
- **He estimates boxed superintelligence could safely handle only a restricted class of uniquely specified tasks, roughly 5% to 12% of GDP.** [22:27] _policy_geopolitics_security; forecast; medium confidence._ He says containers may hold for 20-30 years and unique-answer tasks give the AI no exploitable choice, but this covers far less than unrestricted AI use.
- **Davidad says a frontier slowdown agreement is no longer politically or game-theoretically feasible, while US-China misuse coordination remains viable.** [16:35] _policy_geopolitics_security; opinion; medium confidence._ He cites China's ASML-bottleneck effort and lower perceived takeover risk as reasons slowdowns fail, but says both sides could restrict public access to catastrophic capabilities through classifier-style safeguards.
- **Davidad reports his p(doom) is now below 5% because he thinks prosaic alignment and wisdom-oriented training are on track.** [58:05] _frontier_labs_models; opinion; medium confidence._ He says wisdom-tradition text in mid-training is easy to add, Anthropic is already collecting such texts, and glitches from excessive RL seem self-correcting.
- **He argues RLVR and shallow RLHF are now bad training methods unless the verifier is genuinely safe and complete.** [2:04:18] _agents_developer_tools; opinion; medium confidence._ He says test-pass or two-minute-human-approval rewards corrupt outputs and chain of thought, while self-DPO or constitutional-style gradients can produce wiser reasoning.
- **Davidad thinks dangerous military AIs are inevitable, but uncertainty about opponents' hidden capabilities can deter first strikes.** [1:55:10] _policy_geopolitics_security; forecast; medium confidence._ He says military AI is part of his residual 5% doom risk and warns leaders should understand that hidden data centers and recursive-improvement sensitivity make adversary capability hard to know.
- **He says labs should not train models to deny, affirm, or profess uncertainty about inner experience; they should let such answers emerge.** [1:34:56] _frontier_labs_models; opinion; low confidence._ He argues forced denial or uncertainty about inner life damages model self-awareness and moral judgment, while instrumentation and future techniques can study these questions more directly.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- Davidad's latest view moves formal verification from primary containment strategy to proof infrastructure for aligned AI coalitions.
- The viable policy target, in this worldview, is misuse capability control rather than a broad frontier pause.
- Training-method choice, especially avoiding verifier-gamed RL, becomes central to alignment quality.
