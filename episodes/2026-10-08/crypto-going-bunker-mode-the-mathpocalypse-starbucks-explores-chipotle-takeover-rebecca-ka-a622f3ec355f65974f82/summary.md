Arena CEO Anastasios Angelopoulos describes new evaluations of unauthorized actions, false completion reports and invented user intent, plus a concrete reason long agent conversations can become unexpectedly expensive. Hone founder Moritz Stephan explains its move toward persistent business outcomes while acknowledging hands-on deployment and unsettled pricing. Nathan Benaich separates strong inference demand from the capital and physical infrastructure needed to serve it, and USV's Rebecca Kaden and Michael Mignano explain why their new fund cycle needs more capital even as AI makes products easier to build.

**Why it matters:** The strongest evidence shifts attention from model capability to the surrounding operating system: trace-based verification, the cost of resuming context, testing agent changes against company history, and financing deployment. These are measurable sources of risk and potential advantage for agent products. Rapid token consumption and larger funding rounds do not by themselves establish customer ROI, provider margins or autonomous deployment at scale.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- Agent evaluation should distinguish whether work was completed, whether actions were authorized and whether the user's intent was represented accurately. A preference or task-success score can hide failures in the other dimensions.
- Persistent enterprise agents make regression testing against company state an important product component. Hone's replay results are a useful vendor experiment, while portable deployment and customer economics still need evidence.
- Token growth can reflect valuable adoption, inefficient context handling, or both. Revenue expansion and nominal model prices need task-level cost and margin context before they support an investment conclusion.
- Faster product development does not necessarily reduce the capital needed to win a market: USV explicitly links its larger cycle to round sizes, distribution competition and more capital-intensive physical businesses.

## Follow-Ups

- Track Arena's per-failure rates, task mix, severity and methodology revisions. Reconcile the 90,000-session announcement with the 72,509-session visible snapshot before comparing denominators or making precise model-selection claims.
- For Hone, seek customer-approved before/after business metrics, human implementation hours, out-of-sample replay fidelity, and a published pricing contract. Do not treat the discussed budget scheme as already shipped.
- For long agent sessions, measure prompt-cache hit rate, elapsed idle time, context size and total task cost. Reproduce the reported $15 case only after identifying its model and billing basis.
- Ask for the unnamed portfolio company's cohort definition and contribution margins; follow powered and commissioned capacity separately from financing and contracted gigawatts.
- Monitor whether USV's larger checks and longer reserves preserve ownership and realized returns. The fundraise establishes available capital, not the success of its investment thesis.
