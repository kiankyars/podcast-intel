## Changed Views Or Tensions

- Agent-driven experimentation is already a reported operational workflow at Positron, but faster test execution and high token spending do not show that models independently invent superior hardware architectures.
- Capacity, bandwidth and throughput are separate constraints. Reusing model weights across looped layers can reduce capacity needs while still requiring similar data movement on repeated passes.
- APEX-Agents demonstrates why a rising score can reflect a grading weakness. Comparisons across benchmark versions need the task and judge changes as well as the headline model name.
- Custom software can become easier to generate while the burden of evaluation, integration and maintenance grows. The event-software example supports that narrower claim more strongly than a general collapse of SaaS.
- Anonymous discussions of capability limits are a change in reported sentiment, not a formal commitment. A pretraining cap would also need to account for synthetic-data generation and other ways compute changes capabilities.
