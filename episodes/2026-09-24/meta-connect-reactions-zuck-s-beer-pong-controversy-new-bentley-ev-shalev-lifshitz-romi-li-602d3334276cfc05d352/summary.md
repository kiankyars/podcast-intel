## Changed Views Or Tensions

- Public's reported customer migration suggests agent-based portfolio execution may appeal to sophisticated, high-balance customers early, rather than adoption depending solely on novice investors; one anecdote does not establish a distribution trend.
- TypeSafe's profitability assertion raises a testable commercial question beyond its already-covered technical efficiency thesis, but lacks the accounting detail needed to assess sustainable economics.
- Nanos's account makes availability for a particular customer and timely software support material complements to a cloud's benchmark ranking; even a top-rated provider may be inaccessible at the required date.
- Solcoa's feedstock pivot and Nanos's labor account identify distinct physical limits: scalable material input for a process plant and trained workers for data-center construction and operations. Financing alone does not establish delivered capacity.
