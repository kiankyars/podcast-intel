# Biohub: The Future of Biology is Open-Source with Co-Founders Mark Zuckerberg, Priscilla Chan, and Head of Science Alex Rives

- Podcast: No Priors
- Published: 2026-06-10
- Source: 
- Relevance: 5/5

Biohub's leaders describe a $500 million virtual biology effort that couples frontier AI with new wet-lab measurement systems, then releases the resulting tools as open science. The most concrete update is ESMFold2: Rives says the model folded 1.1 billion proteins, hit state-of-the-art structure benchmarks, and generated nanomolar binders from a small lab validation cycle.

**Why it matters:** The episode frames biological AI as a data-generation and institutional-design problem, not just a model-scaling problem. If Biohub's open-source protein and cell world models work, they could become shared infrastructure for drug discovery and synthetic biology rather than proprietary biotech point products.
