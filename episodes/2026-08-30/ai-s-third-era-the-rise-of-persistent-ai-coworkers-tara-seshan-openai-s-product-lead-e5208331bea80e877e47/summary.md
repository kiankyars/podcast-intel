# AI’s third era: the rise of persistent AI coworkers | Tara Seshan (OpenAI’s product lead)

- Podcast: Lenny's Podcast
- Published: 2026-08-30
- Source: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/ais-third-era-the-rise-of-persistent
- Relevance: 5/5

OpenAI product lead Tara Seshan gives a substantive first-party account of how Codex and ChatGPT Work are built and where the product is heading. She says OpenAI targets model capabilities two to three months ahead, describes Work as Codex with a knowledge-worker-oriented interface, and names automatic model-and-harness routing as the consolidation goal. She also identifies data access, cloud infrastructure, reliability, process visibility, and citations as critical constraints on persistent agents and knowledge-work adoption. Her product examples suggest OpenAI is extending Codex from coding into financial modeling and prompt-built, hosted personal software, although most adoption claims remain qualitative and company-reported.

**Why it matters:** The interview materially clarifies OpenAI's application-layer strategy: Codex is not merely a coding product but an agent engine being repackaged for broader knowledge work, with the long-term interface expected to hide model and harness choices. It also shifts the diligence focus from raw model intelligence toward routing, permissions, connectors, reliability, collaboration, and inspectable work processes. Those layers may determine whether increasingly capable models become dependable enterprise coworkers and whether ChatGPT can distribute agentic workflows beyond developers.

## Changed Views Or Tensions

- ChatGPT Work and Codex appear to be interface variants over substantially the same agent capability rather than separate underlying product stacks.
- OpenAI's application teams optimize around a narrow two-to-three-month model-development horizon and explicitly couple product roadmaps to research priorities.
- The intended end state is less user-visible model and mode choice, with automatic routing across models and agent harnesses.
- For enterprise knowledge work, provenance and inspectable intermediate work may be as important as final-answer quality because many outputs lack deterministic tests.
- Cloud-agent adoption may be bottlenecked by data access, permissions, connectors, and reliability even when model intelligence is sufficient.
- OpenAI is positioning the Codex engine beyond coding, including financial workflows and prompt-built hosted personal software.

## Follow-Ups

- Benchmark identical financial-modeling and research tasks in Codex and ChatGPT Work to test Seshan's claim of capability parity and isolate interface-induced differences.
- Track when ChatGPT begins automatically routing tasks across chat, Work, and Codex harnesses, and measure whether routing improves completion quality without obscuring user control.
- Audit ChatGPT Work's citation, input, and intermediate-work visibility on consequential knowledge tasks, including whether a reviewer can reconstruct and challenge the model's reasoning path.
- Map the connectors, permission model, audit logs, failure recovery, and long-running-task reliability needed for OpenAI's persistent coworker vision in a real enterprise deployment.
- Verify Sites' database, hosting, sharing, privacy, internal-data, and auto-update behavior, then compare retention and maintenance burden with documents, spreadsheets, and conventional internal-tool builders.
- Look for quantitative evidence behind the corporate-finance adoption example, including task frequency, time saved, error rates, reviewer effort, and whether previously concentrated expertise genuinely diffused across the team.
