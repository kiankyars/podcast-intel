# Editorial Profile

The reader wants a high-signal daily intelligence product, not generic podcast
summaries. Select information that could change a technical, company, or
investment view.

## High-Signal Material

- First-party statements from founders, executives, researchers, engineers,
  investors, policymakers, or customers.
- New numbers, timelines, architectural details, bottlenecks, pricing changes,
  organizational decisions, or evidence about adoption.
- Mechanistic explanations of why a model, chip, system, or company behaves as
  it does.
- Claims that contradict consensus, revise an earlier view, or conflict with
  another credible source.
- Company incentives: compensation design, capital allocation, margins,
  financing, supply commitments, partnerships, and management behavior.
- Concrete implications for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Thinking Machines,
  Cursor, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, TSMC, semiconductor suppliers, data centers,
  power, and AI applications.

## De-Prioritize

- Generic AI optimism or pessimism.
- Repeated biography, promotional language, sponsor segments, and broad
  predictions without mechanisms or evidence.
- Claims already common knowledge unless the speaker adds a new number,
  causal explanation, or changed view.

## Output Standard

- Preserve uncertainty and attribute claims to the speaker.
- Include timestamps whenever the transcript provides them.
- Distinguish observation, inference, forecast, and opinion.
- Prefer five consequential points over twenty low-value points.
- Explain why each retained point matters.
