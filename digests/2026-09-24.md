### [Ep. 033 - ClusterMAX 3.0 Is Here! Neoclouds Ranked (Neoclouds, GPUs) | Sam Harshe, Pratt Bhatt, Jordan Nanos](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/jordan-nanos/episodes/Ep--033---ClusterMAX-3-0-Is-Here--Neoclouds-Ranked-Neoclouds--GPUs--Sam-Harshe--Pratt-Bhatt--Jordan-Nanos-e3pa84t)

SemiAnalysis Weekly · Relevance 4/5

SemiAnalysis's ClusterMAX 3.0 discussion puts operational quality ahead of GPU specifications: failure recovery, network configuration and software integration determine how much useful work customers receive. Its updated managed-cluster ratings add Nebius to Platinum alongside CoreWeave and Google Cloud to Gold alongside Oracle. The team separately reports preliminary endpoint tests in which poorer cache-hit rates nearly doubled replay cost. Financing discussion connects technical failures to delayed acceptance and contract cancellation, while older-GPU demand and the complexity of hosted RL complicate simple hardware-obsolescence and managed-service narratives.

**Why it matters:** The useful distinction is between selling nominal compute and delivering reliable, economical workloads. Scarcity can sustain sales even for weaker operators, while recovery behavior and network integration change effective cost and contractual risk. ClusterMAX is an evaluator's assessment of managed GPU clusters, not a ranking of every cloud product; endpoint and hosted-training work is separate. SemiAnalysis also sells related research and technical advisory services, and the podcast's market and financing claims are not audited financial disclosures.

### [Re-Founding Incumbents for the AI Era with Sequence Holdings Co-Founder and CEO Michael Lee](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpRwJBQvW0)

No Priors · Relevance 4/5

Sequence Holdings CEO Michael Lee describes an ownership-and-engineering model for applying AI inside established businesses, with BankSouth providing the most concrete operating evidence. He reports a 94% reduction in consumer underwriting time and a fall in the bank's average end-to-end loan turnaround from 30 to 11 days. He also says the bank absorbed doubled quarterly loan volume with a smaller underwriting team, while explicitly denying that Sequence caused the demand increase. Sequence plans to extend the Atlas platform developed at the bank to Baldwin through an announced $7.7 billion take-private with the Dell family office. BankSouth results are founder-reported; cross-industry reuse, financial returns and Baldwin transformation results remain unproven in this interview. Host Sarah Guo discloses that she is a Sequence investor.

**Why it matters:** The useful evidence is a specific mechanism for enterprise AI adoption: automate routine loan processing, reserve underwriters for exceptions and absorb more volume through existing centralized operations. The case makes organizational structure, access to business data and authority to change workflows relevant to AI investment selection. It also suggests that a services engagement can precede an ownership partnership. The missing baselines, deployment costs and credit outcomes prevent translating the reported cycle-time improvements directly into ROI or assuming the approach will transfer to insurance brokerage.
