# 2026-11-11

## TL;DR

- **Tigges reports that the model preserves substantial capability when only its strongest routed MLP activations are retained for each token.** [27:14; 28:46; 30:28; 1:23:04] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/the-neuron-basis-goodfire-s-curt-tigges-on-agentic-interp-monosemanticity-reverse-engineering/)). He describes near-baseline non-reasoning performance under heavy dynamic ablation. The [published study](https://www.goodfire.com/research/neurons-as-an-interpretable-basis#router-weighted-top-k-filtering) specifies retaining 320 of 4,096 routed neurons per layer per token, suppressing 92.2%; shared-expert neurons remain unchanged. This is a changing activation mask, not permanent neuron removal or a measured inference-speed gain.
- **Rocha says Resend’s MCP usage reached roughly 1.2 million calls in August, while building a remote service required revisiting OAuth, roles and resource permissions rather than merely wrapping the send-email API.** [00:37:56; 00:38:46; 00:38:56; 00:40:11; 00:40:26; 00:43:12; 00:43:35; 00:43:56; 00:46:34; 01:09:19] ([Latent Space](https://lspace.beehiiv.com/podcast/s/latent_space_the_ai_engineer_podcast/designing_for_agents_zeno_rocha_resend)). He describes recent monthly doubling and faster growth than the CLI, without supplying unique-user, retention or revenue figures. The MCP expanded from roughly 20 tools to more than 80 at recording. He identifies response size and coverage of billing, team invitations, provisioning and deletion as difficult design questions; a larger tool count is not a demonstrated success-rate improvement. The [current official MCP documentation](https://resend.com/docs/mcp-server) corroborates hosted OAuth and broad management capabilities. Separately, the [September 22 headless-dashboard initiative](https://resend.com/blog/headless-dashboard-initiative) describes programmatic coverage as ongoing work. Rocha remains undecided about unauthenticated provisioning because of abuse risk.
- **Goodfire's Neuron Scientist tests and revises neuron explanations, then evaluates whether another model can reconstruct their causal effects.** [41:31; 44:02; 45:59; 48:06; 51:09] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/the-neuron-basis-goodfire-s-curt-tigges-on-agentic-interp-monosemanticity-reverse-engineering/)). Tigges describes counterfactual text experiments and independent prediction feedback; one difficult neuron tracked a reformatted list introduced far earlier in the context. Predicted activations are patched into the model after ablation. The interview cites 74% damage recovery; the [published simulation result](https://www.goodfire.com/research/neurons-as-an-interpretable-basis#simulation) is 73% of the perfect-reader recovery ceiling in the strongest activation regime. This measures scoped single-neuron reconstruction, not the fraction of the whole model understood.

Goodfire’s experimental results retain their model and evaluation scope; Resend’s usage figures are recording-time claims, and its October product launches are verified separately from the interview’s roadmap discussion.

## More findings

- **Tigges says 100 million tokens of post-training restored sparse-model reasoning to parity or better on the benchmarks tested.** [1:37:01; 1:37:07; 1:39:55; 1:44:19] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/the-neuron-basis-goodfire-s-curt-tigges-on-agentic-interp-monosemanticity-reverse-engineering/)). He corrects the host's 10-million-token figure to 100 million and describes loops and excessive self-checking before recovery. The [paper](https://www.goodfire.com/research/neurons-as-an-interpretable-basis#fine-tuning-for-performance-recovery) specifies 91M SFT plus 9M RL tokens, with all weights trained under the mask. Tigges interprets recovery as recalibration of largely retained knowledge; the mechanism is unproven. Coding tests reached 32k context, while million-token behavior remains untested.
- **Tigges describes gated MLP neurons as context-dependent computations whose distinct meanings can feed different downstream readers.** [12:26; 18:04; 59:18; 1:04:03] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/the-neuron-basis-goodfire-s-curt-tigges-on-agentic-interp-monosemanticity-reverse-engineering/)). He explains how separate gate and up projections combine signals and how context prepares different readers for a neuron associated with oral health in one setting and marine wildlife in another. Positive and negative activations can also have different meanings. His proposed target is an enumerable set of computational roles, including reasoning control signals, rather than requiring every neuron to encode one human concept.
- **Tigges reports that some difficult-to-interpret double-negative activations can rescue reasoning even when their sequence information is disrupted.** [1:33:32] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/the-neuron-basis-goodfire-s-curt-tigges-on-agentic-interp-monosemanticity-reverse-engineering/)). When both SwiGLU branches are negative, their product is positive. Tigges says removing these activations sometimes damages reasoning, yet restoring them in reversed token order or from another sequence can repair it. He explicitly leaves their mechanism unresolved. This is a concrete limitation on interpreting weak, incoherent activations as dispensable noise.

## Episodes

### [The Neuron Basis: Goodfire's Curt Tigges on Agentic Interp, Monosemanticity & Reverse Engineering](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/the-neuron-basis-goodfire-s-curt-tigges-on-agentic-interp-monosemanticity-reverse-engineering/)

The Cognitive Revolution · Relevance 4/5

Goodfire's Curt Tigges presents experiments on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B that make individual MLP neurons more credible units for interpretability: strong activations carry much of the tested behavior, modest post-training repairs sparse-model reasoning, and an agent's neuron descriptions support causal reconstruction tests. Gated neurons can participate in several context-specific computations. These results strengthen the experimental case beyond the earlier clean-neurons essay, while leaving long-context behavior, weak-activation mechanisms and whole-model understanding unresolved.

**Why it matters:** The practical shift is toward testing explanations directly against the original model's neurons and their effects, potentially reducing reliance on learned replacement features for MLP analysis. The evidence supports a tractable research program, not a completed model audit: Tigges explicitly distinguishes a neuron inventory from understanding interacting circuits, attention and the residual stream. Monitoring or safety benefits still require behavior-specific validation.

### [Designing for Agents — Zeno Rocha, Resend](https://lspace.beehiiv.com/podcast/s/latent_space_the_ai_engineer_podcast/designing_for_agents_zeno_rocha_resend)

Latent Space · Relevance 4/5

Zeno Rocha explains how Resend is adapting an email infrastructure business to agents as both users and distribution channels. The useful evidence concerns rising MCP activity, the permissions and API work behind a headless product, machine-readable pricing, and expansion into owned sending infrastructure and agent inboxes. Commercial counts are company-reported snapshots, not revenue or market-share measures. Published November 10, the interview refers to August activity and the day of an Astra launch without establishing an exact recording date; product announcements checked separately show that two discussed roadmap items launched in October. Host swyx discloses that he is an investor in Resend.

**Why it matters:** Agent access is becoming part of the product itself: authentication, account operations, readable commercial information and persistent inbox state all affect whether an agent can discover and use a service. Resend also illustrates a developer-experience business moving into infrastructure ownership. The interview provides concrete implementation choices and adoption claims, while leaving the economics and causal impact of its distribution tactics unmeasured.
