### [AI’s third era: the rise of persistent AI coworkers | Tara Seshan (OpenAI’s product lead)](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/ais-third-era-the-rise-of-persistent)

**Lenny's Podcast | relevance 5/5**

OpenAI product lead Tara Seshan gives a substantive first-party account of how Codex and ChatGPT Work are built and where the product is heading. She says OpenAI targets model capabilities two to three months ahead, describes Work as Codex with a knowledge-worker-oriented interface, and names automatic model-and-harness routing as the consolidation goal. She also identifies data access, cloud infrastructure, reliability, process visibility, and citations as critical constraints on persistent agents and knowledge-work adoption. Her product examples suggest OpenAI is extending Codex from coding into financial modeling and prompt-built, hosted personal software, although most adoption claims remain qualitative and company-reported.

**Why it matters:** The interview materially clarifies OpenAI's application-layer strategy: Codex is not merely a coding product but an agent engine being repackaged for broader knowledge work, with the long-term interface expected to hide model and harness choices. It also shifts the diligence focus from raw model intelligence toward routing, permissions, connectors, reliability, collaboration, and inspectable work processes. Those layers may determine whether increasingly capable models become dependable enterprise coworkers and whether ChatGPT can distribute agentic workflows beyond developers.
