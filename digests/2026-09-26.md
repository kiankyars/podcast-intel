## Episodes

### [OpenRouter: from Seed to Stripe — with OpenRouter’s Alex Atallah & AMP’s Anjney Midha](https://www.latent.space/p/openrouter)

Latent Space · Relevance 4/5

OpenRouter co-founder Alex Atallah and investor Anjney Midha explain the platform's growth, model-fusion experiments and planned combination with Stripe. The strongest operating claims are token volume growing roughly 9% week over week from more than 10 trillion tokens per day, and a tenfold month-over-month increase in the dollar volume of abuse blocked. They describe cheap agent heartbeat routing as a new demand driver and security as a central rationale for partnering with Stripe. The publisher transcript repeatedly swaps the guests' labels; operating claims are therefore attributed by role rather than by its unreliable speaker labels.

**Why it matters:** A model gateway's economics depend on traffic quality, fraud control and how efficiently it routes heterogeneous workloads. Rising token throughput alone does not establish revenue growth, margins or productive agent adoption. The episode identifies concrete pressures on the gateway business and a model-fusion product hypothesis that still needs independent, cost-adjusted evaluation.

### [Zero to One in AI Safety: Halcyon's Mike McCormick on Launching 30 New Orgs & the Founder Bottleneck](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/zero-to-one-in-ai-safety-halcyon-s-mike-mccormick-on-launching-30-new-orgs-the-founder-bottleneck/)

The Cognitive Revolution · Relevance 3/5

Halcyon founder Mike McCormick describes an organization-building model that combines career-transition grants with subsequent nonprofit funding or venture investment. He reports roughly 30 organizations launched over three years and about $500 million raised collectively, and explains how grants can support founders before they select a project. The most useful additions are early investments in verifiable inference and compute infrastructure, a focus on projects that could matter within one to four years, and demand for experienced operating leaders. The AIUC product overview repeats September 17 coverage; broad catastrophe timelines, consciousness speculation and prospective safety-market valuations are not retained as new evidence.

**Why it matters:** The interview identifies a concrete allocation mechanism for expanding the independent AI safety ecosystem: finance experienced people through project selection, then help them recruit and launch. It also connects verification research to a potential infrastructure requirement for enforcing model and compute commitments. Halcyon's reported organization count and aggregate fundraising describe ecosystem formation, not its own assets under management, investment returns or demonstrated reductions in risk. McCormick is an interested funder; product maturity, grant conversion rates and the relative severity of capital versus talent constraints remain unmeasured here.
