# 2026-10-25

## TL;DR

- **The analysts infer that ASML could apply lithography-grade positioning and metrology to a track-based hybrid bonder that brings the wafer to a die held on a carrier.** [00:38; 01:31; 02:16; 03:04; 13:32; 14:15] ([SemiAnalysis Weekly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4WCasOsS5-4)). They describe dies staying on holders through preparation and measurement before bonding. ASML's [WO2025201788A1 application](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2025201788A1/en), published October 2, 2025, corroborates this design family, while permitting different positioning arrangements; it does not require that only the wafer moves. The speakers put commercialization several years away. Counterpoint: [Besi's October 22 release, pp. 2–3](https://www.besi.com/fileadmin/user_upload/PR_Q3-2026.pdf), says it does not view ASML as a competitor, understands ASML's work as longer-term transistor stacking, and describes joint die-stress work. Neither the patent nor these competing interpretations establishes a commercial product or displacement of Besi.
- **Conrad says SF Compute has not yet reduced a startup's required fundraising; collateral credibility will take years to build.** [41:54; 42:21; 43:30; 45:49; 46:01] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/ai-am-highlights-clean-neurons-black-october-the-gpu-credit-crunch-and-buying-the-supply-chain/)). He connects long commitments and deposits to lenders' dependence on customer credit, while acknowledging the present limitation at 45:49. SF Compute's [September commercial announcement](https://sfcompute.com/news/tcv-q3-2026) separately links financing to multiyear commitments and describes resale as recovery of unused capacity spending. Its [marketplace terms](https://sfcompute.com/legal/terms-of-service) require purchases to be prefunded or paid by card. Neither establishes lower collateral haircuts or easier startup credit. This sharpens the earlier financing thesis without claiming it has already worked.
- **The move toward larger High-NA photomasks is a coordinated mask-ecosystem transition, with official targets of a 2031 pilot line and production-system readiness in 2033.** [25:35; 26:01; 26:31; 27:08; 28:19; 28:48; 29:15] ([SemiAnalysis Weekly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4WCasOsS5-4)). The speakers explain how doubling one mask dimension could restore a full exposure field under High-NA's asymmetric reduction and reduce stitching constraints. They identify mask production, inspection, repair, pellicles and handling as dependencies. The [September 8 ASML–TSMC announcement](https://investor.asml.com/news-releases/news-release-details/asml-and-tsmc-announce-initiative-pioneer-industry-transition-large-format-photomasks-high-na-euv) supplies the 2031/2033 targets and explicitly says High-NA production starts with current six-inch masks. Larger masks therefore are not a prerequisite for initial High-NA adoption. The transcript's garbled year and area units should not be used as specifications; no retrofit compatibility or realized productivity gain is established here.

## More findings

- **SemiAnalysis forecasts that high-volume die-to-wafer hybrid bonding for HBM will wait until 2029 or later because stack-density gains do not yet justify the added cost and qualification burden.** [07:38; 07:56; 08:55; 09:27; 09:56; 10:21; 11:04; 11:20] ([SemiAnalysis Weekly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4WCasOsS5-4)). Howie contrasts logic's connectivity benefits with HBM's incentive to fit more memory layers into a package, and says customers have requested fewer layers at a given performance target. Zhang adds accuracy and throughput constraints. In contrast, [Besi's October 22 release, p. 2](https://www.besi.com/fileadmin/user_upload/PR_Q3-2026.pdf), anticipates hybrid bonding across HBM stack heights within two years. These are competing expectations, with potentially different adoption thresholds; neither provides a committed volume schedule or comparable cost-per-stack and yield data. Evaluation-tool orders are not equivalent to volume memory production.
- **Conrad describes resale obligations and priority for small jobs as mechanisms to prevent capacity hoarding.** [47:54; 48:22; 48:50] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/ai-am-highlights-clean-neurons-black-october-the-gpu-credit-crunch-and-buying-the-supply-chain/)). His Harberger-style contract account lacks complete published terms. Separately, the [current Autoresearch product page](https://autoresearch.sfcompute.com/) specifies first-node priority ahead of paid tiers, allocation favoring the least-served customer, an 85% ceiling on batch use of the fleet, and capacity held for returning users. Those policies support access for experimentation; they do not guarantee instant allocation of any requested cluster or prove deep secondary-market liquidity.
- **The proposed architecture shifts the commercial test from positioning accuracy alone to maintaining accuracy, cleanliness and throughput across an integrated moving system.** [14:43; 15:24; 15:47; 15:57; 17:05; 18:24; 19:18] ([SemiAnalysis Weekly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4WCasOsS5-4)). Howie asks how measurement modules and process environments would engage dies on the track without degrading precision or introducing particles. Zhang connects alignment and contamination to bonding yield and usable throughput. A particle can prevent contact over an area larger than the contaminant itself, so a good measurement at one station is insufficient if the surface is recontaminated before bonding. The [patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2025201788A1/en) proposes isolation and vibration-control arrangements; these are design provisions, not qualification results. No comparative yield, uptime, dies-per-hour or cost-per-good-bond measurements are supplied.
- **Labenz separates privileged assistants from autonomous agents and reports approval fatigue from false-positive data-sharing alerts.** [1:10:45; 1:11:54; 1:12:50] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/ai-am-highlights-clean-neurons-black-october-the-gpu-credit-crunch-and-buying-the-supply-chain/)). His HelpPeer setup pairs personal-data access with low autonomy, and separate accounts with greater autonomy. This is an early personal experiment, without measured leakage protection or reliable inter-agent cooperation. Evaluate alert precision and actual data boundaries before assuming the architecture is secure.
- **Conrad attributes apparent hyperscaler price premiums to comparing on-demand capacity with reserved contracts.** [46:25; 46:39] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/ai-am-highlights-clean-neurons-black-october-the-gpu-credit-crunch-and-buying-the-supply-chain/)). At 46:39 he describes the cost of idle capacity and customer flexibility. No matched hardware, service or contract comparison is supplied, so price parity remains his interpretation. Compare term, interruption rights, utilization and delivered work before treating headline GPU-hour prices as evidence of provider efficiency.
- **Mowshowitz distinguishes more readable neurons from predicting or explaining whole-model behavior.** [16:34; 19:34; 23:48] ([The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/ai-am-highlights-clean-neurons-black-october-the-gpu-credit-crunch-and-buying-the-supply-chain/)). Goodfire's [original essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/) presents a working hypothesis, acknowledges incomplete auditing, and permits grouped meanings through recursive generalization. A neuron with multiple responses therefore does not alone refute its current formulation. The useful test is whether explanations predict interventions and behavior on held-out cases. Neither the episode nor the essay establishes solved interpretability or alignment.
- **The analysts argue that fine-grained logic partitioning across bonded layers gives China another way to improve performance under process constraints, while increasing dependence on packaging yield and design tools.** [20:41; 21:36; 24:21; 24:48; 30:19; 31:11] ([SemiAnalysis Weekly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4WCasOsS5-4)). Howie describes moving memory and selected functions onto another tier so timing-critical blocks can sit closer together, shortening communication paths. He expects more aggressive stacking to create additional bonding and yield demands. [Huawei's May LogicFolding announcement](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2026/5/ieee-iscas-tau-scaling) supports the critical-path rationale, not independently measured parity with leading process nodes. The suggestion that Western EDA has never addressed such partitioning is too broad: [Cadence's platform documentation](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/resources/datasheets/integrity-3d-ic-platform-ds.html) already describes netlist partitioning, logic-on-logic exploration and cross-die analysis. Neither source establishes export-control immunity or interchangeable manufacturing flows.

### [Ep. 040 - ASML Is Building a Hybrid Bonding Tool, and Its Patents Show How (AI Supply Chain) | Jordan Nanos, Howie, Sherry Zhang](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/jordan-nanos/episodes/Ep--040---ASML-Is-Building-a-Hybrid-Bonding-Tool--and-Its-Patents-Show-How-AI-Supply-Chain--Jordan-Nanos--Howie--Sherry-Zhang-e3qulph)

SemiAnalysis Weekly · Relevance 5/5

SemiAnalysis's Jordan Nanos, Howie and Sherry Zhang examine an ASML patent for a track-based hybrid-bonding system, a possible extension of its metrology and wafer-stage capabilities. The useful distinction is between a disclosed technical concept and a commercially qualified tool. They identify integration, cleanliness and yield as gates, forecast HBM volume adoption in 2029 or later, and explain the larger-mask transition for High-NA. Besi's October 22 management statement disputes both the competitive interpretation and the HBM timing. Their China discussion supplies a mechanism for performance gains through three-dimensional partitioning, but does not establish equivalent process technology or superior production economics.

**Why it matters:** Hybrid bonding creates opportunities across alignment, inspection, cleaning and die handling, while its value depends on application-specific economics. Logic stacking, HBM and larger High-NA masks have different adoption schedules. Treating them as one imminent equipment cycle would obscure the important risks: customer qualification, usable throughput, bonding yield, mask infrastructure and whether existing packaging methods remain sufficient.
