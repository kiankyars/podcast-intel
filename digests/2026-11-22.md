## TL;DR

- **Buchanan estimates an allied advantage of roughly 100 to 1 in producing computing power and 15 to 1 in access to it.** He identifies the US and its allies as one side of the comparison and China as the other. In his account of Biden-era strategy, restricting access to advanced compute could give domestic labs room to pace development while remaining ahead. The interview supplies neither the underlying chip inventory nor a conversion from these ratios to months of model-development lead. [The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/spend-the-lead-on-safety-biden-ai-advisors-ben-buchanan-tantum-collins-on-the-fate-of-the-world/) [29:02; 34:31; 56:05]
- **Buchanan and Collins say months of diplomacy yielded a narrow US–China commitment on human control over the decision to use nuclear weapons.** Both advisers participated in the May 2024 Geneva talks. Buchanan recalls disagreement over shared AI risks and over whether chip sales should precede further talks. Collins describes the work needed to agree on precise language by November. The Chinese government's account of the Lima meeting confirms the narrow scope of the public commitment. Their description of bargaining positions is retrospective. [Lima readout](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/eng/xw/fyrbt/202411/t20241117_11527715.html) [The Cognitive Revolution](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/spend-the-lead-on-safety-biden-ai-advisors-ben-buchanan-tantum-collins-on-the-fate-of-the-world/) [41:53; 43:59; 59:35; 1:00:07]
- **Buchanan proposes tying further frontier development to safety and security thresholds because compute can accumulate during a fixed pause.** A calendar-based restart could put a much larger pool of hardware behind the next capability jump. Buchanan proposes predeployment testing by government or independent auditors and embedded evaluators during development. Collins says formal disclosures and direct conversations gave officials visibility into lab progress. Neither guest specifies a quantitative threshold or an agreed verification protocol. The Cognitive Revolution · Relevance 4/5

Former White House AI advisers Ben Buchanan and Tantum Collins explain the intended connection between chip export controls, diplomacy with China and oversight of frontier labs. They expected allied control of compute production to buy time for safety work. Their account of the 2024 talks shows how much negotiation it took to reach even a narrow point of agreement.
