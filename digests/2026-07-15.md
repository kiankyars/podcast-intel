**Lenny's Podcast | relevance 5/5 | [local summary](../episodes/2026-07-09/adam-mosseri-ai-is-a-tailwind-for-authenticity-5476a282eca641a33094/summary.md)**

Adam Mosseri describes Instagram's adoption of smaller generalist product pods and a new product-staff role spanning PM, design, research, and data work, while arguing that engineering broadly is becoming more centered on planning and review. He also outlines emerging token-budget economics and an Instagram strategy that uses language models to make recommenders more legible while treating creator identity and content provenance as more important in a synthetic-media world.

**Why it matters:** This is first-party evidence that AI is changing both the operating model and cost structure of a three-billion-user product, not merely adding features. Instagram is reducing coordination overhead, treating model spend as a future labor-scale resource, and preparing for a content ecosystem where ranking, provenance, and trusted identity matter more than reliably detecting every generated artifact.
