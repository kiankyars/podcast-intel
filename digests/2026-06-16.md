## TL;DR

Their modeling says reallocating 5% of 2026 N3 smartphone wafers yields only slightly above 100,000 Rubin GPUs or 300,000 TPU v7s; even 25% reallocation yields about 700,000 Rubin GPUs and 1.5 million TPU v7s.
