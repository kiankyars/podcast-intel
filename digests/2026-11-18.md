### [Goldman Sachs CEO Reacts to Jensen Huang's Potential Return to NVIDIA](https://share.transistor.fm/s/9ad0e6dc)

TBPN · Relevance 3/5

Goldman Sachs CEO David Solomon gives a concrete account of AI-assisted cyber defense: access to powerful models for seven to eight months and thousands to tens of thousands of vulnerability scans each day. He says the firm is more secure, while warning that rapid patching can itself disrupt systems. He also identifies less-resourced vendors' security capabilities as a weak point and proposes a public-private effort to upgrade legacy software. On commercial strategy, he expects technology efficiencies to support broader institutional and ultra-high-net-worth client coverage over five to ten years. These are operating claims and intentions, without quantified security outcomes or AI savings.

**Why it matters:** The useful evidence is an incumbent's reported deployment scale and the constraints around it: discovering vulnerabilities is only part of the job; safe patch rollout and weaker suppliers also matter. Solomon's client-segment boundary clarifies where Goldman expects AI-enabled capacity to go. Track remediation outcomes, operational incidents and incremental client coverage to distinguish activity from economic benefit.
